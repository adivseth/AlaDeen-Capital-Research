TermDefinition
DCF / FCFFDiscounted cash flow; free cash flow to the firm = NOPAT + D&A − capex − change in working capital, discounted at WACC
WACCWeighted average cost of capital (equity and debt)
Terminal valueValue beyond the explicit forecast; NOPAT × (1 − g/ROC) ÷ (WACC − g)
EBITDA / EBITOperating profit before / after depreciation and amortisation
ROE / ROCEPAT ÷ average equity; (PBT + interest) ÷ average (equity + borrowings)
CFO / PATOperating cash flow ÷ profit; below 0.7 for years is an earnings-quality warning
Margin of safety(Intrinsic value − price) ÷ intrinsic value (Graham)
